    FREQUENTLY ASKED
    Quick answers
    Is my message content stored?
    Only an anonymized snippet is stored, and only for messages flagged High or Critical risk. Full raw content is never persisted beyond generating your result.
    Which languages are supported?
    English, Hindi, and Tamil — both for detection and for the explanation you receive.
    Is this connected to my real Gmail, Outlook, or SMS?
    Not yet. Paste content in manually for now — live account integration is a planned next step.
    What if I think a result is wrong?
    Submit it through the form above — we review false positives and false negatives to keep improving detection.
